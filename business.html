FAQ

Questions before you export.

Can WA Exporter train AI agents directly?

WA Exporter exports available WhatsApp Web conversations into supported files. You can prepare appropriately authorized exports for third-party AI tools or chatbot training. WA Exporter does not train models itself; remove personal information before sharing data.

Can I analyze all business chats at once?

WA Exporter PRO supports selecting multiple available chats and exporting them. The amount of history available depends on your WhatsApp Web session, the selected date range and supported export behavior. Analyze the exported files using your preferred tools.

Can I export WhatsApp group contacts in bulk?

WA Exporter PRO supports exporting visible group-member and contact data to CSV or vCard where available. Only export data you are authorized to access and use, and obtain appropriate consent for marketing outreach.

Is my business conversation data private?

Export generation is local-first in your browser. The existing product information states that chat and contact export content is not sent to Dodo Payments. Treat downloaded files as sensitive and review third-party tools' privacy policies before uploading them.
