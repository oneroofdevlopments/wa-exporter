Troubleshooting

Help us diagnose the issue

These details are usually enough to start investigating without sharing private conversations:

  • WA Exporter version, Chrome version and operating system.
  • Which operation you tried: chat export, multiple chats, contacts, group members or license activation.
  • Chosen output format (such as TXT, CSV, vCard, HTML, PDF or ZIP) and date range, if relevant.
  • Where the process stopped and the exact error message, if one appeared.
  • A screenshot only if needed, with names, numbers, messages, group details and activation codes removed.

Keep your information private

Never send us passwords, WhatsApp QR codes, payment-card details, full private chats, contact lists, group-member lists or complete license keys.

WhatsApp messages and contact exports are processed locally in your browser. Contacting support is optional and separate from the export process; anything you choose to send in a support message is shared through that communication channel.

Quick answers

Before you contact us

How do I cancel or manage PRO?

Open Manage PRO and use the payment provider's secure customer portal. You do not need to send us your payment-card details.

How can I move my PRO license to another computer?

In the WA Exporter PRO screen, choose Deactivate on your current device, then activate your valid license on your other device. Deactivating the device does not cancel the subscription.

Can you recover missing chat history or hidden group contacts?

We can help with export troubleshooting, but WA Exporter can only work with data available to your authenticated WhatsApp Web session. It cannot bypass account permissions or guarantee recovery of unavailable content.

Where should I report a security issue?

Report it privately to support@waexporter.in. Share only the minimum information needed to describe the issue, and avoid posting vulnerabilities or personal data publicly.
