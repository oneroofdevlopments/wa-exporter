How to export multiple WhatsApp chats

WA Exporter PRO includes a multi-chat workflow for conversations available in your authenticated WhatsApp Web session.

Plan requirement

Multi-chat export is a PRO feature. The current site lists PRO at $7.99/month for one active device and includes multi-chat export, additional formats, custom date ranges and resume support.

1. Sign in and load WhatsApp Web

Use WhatsApp's normal device-linking process. WA Exporter works with information available in the signed-in browser session and does not bypass account permissions.

2. Select conversations

Open WA Exporter's full workflow and select the chats you need. Keep the selection limited to conversations you are authorized to access and export.

3. Apply the export settings

Choose the available output format and date controls for the job. For a specific period, use the custom date controls included with PRO.

4. Let required history load

Large or older exports can take longer because WhatsApp Web may need to load additional history. Content that is unavailable in the current session cannot be recovered by WA Exporter.

5. Recover from interruption

If an eligible PRO export is interrupted, use the resume feature where supported rather than starting the entire workflow again.

Verify the result

Open the downloaded files and confirm the expected conversations and date ranges are present before relying on the export as an archive.
