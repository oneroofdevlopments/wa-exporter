WhatsApp PDF export guide

How to export a WhatsApp chat to PDF

Save a readable copy of a conversation from your signed-in WhatsApp Web session using WA Exporter.

Export a WhatsApp Web conversation as a PDF

  1. Install WA Exporter. Get the Chrome extension.
  2. Open WhatsApp Web. Sign in normally and select a conversation you have permission to export.
  3. Select PDF. Open WA Exporter, select your conversation and choose PDF as the output format. Check the available features for your plan.
  4. Choose a date range. Select the available history or an appropriate date filter.
  5. Export and save. Generate the file and save it locally through Chrome.

Why export WhatsApp chats to PDF?

PDF can be useful when you want a readable reference copy of a conversation for personal records, project documentation or an authorized business workflow. It is not a replacement for WhatsApp's own account backup or restore features.

Can you export multiple chats?

WA Exporter offers multi-chat export workflows on supported plans. Review the Free and PRO options to confirm which export formats and limits apply.

Does WA Exporter access deleted or hidden messages?

No. WA Exporter works from content available in your authenticated WhatsApp Web session. It does not bypass login, permissions or encryption, and it cannot guarantee access to history not available in that session.

Is the PDF created locally?

WA Exporter is designed for local-first processing of export content in your browser. Only export information you are authorized to access and handle it responsibly.
