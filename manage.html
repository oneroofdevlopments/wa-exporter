Manage WA Exporter PRO

Private self-service subscription and device management

Manage billing or cancel your subscription

Billing and subscription changes are handled directly by Dodo Payments, our payment provider. WA Exporter does not ask you to enter payment-card details on this website.

Open secure Dodo Customer Portal →

Use the same email address you used when purchasing WA Exporter PRO. Dodo will authenticate you before showing subscription information.

What you can manage there

  • View your active WA Exporter PRO subscription and renewal information.
  • Cancel at the next billing date or cancel immediately, where available.
  • Update the payment method for an active or on-hold subscription.
  • View billing history and invoices.
  • Retrieve license keys associated with your purchase.

Moving to another device?

Do not cancel your subscription just to change computers. In the WA Exporter extension, open PRO and choose Deactivate. This releases the current device activation so the same valid license can be activated on another device, subject to the one-device limit.

Privacy and security

  • WA Exporter does not collect the email address you enter into the Dodo Customer Portal.
  • WA Exporter does not receive or store your payment-card number.
  • WhatsApp chats, contacts, group data and exported files are not sent to the customer portal.
  • No private Dodo API key is embedded in this page or required for this self-service flow.

Need help?

Email support@waexporter.in. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or full private conversations.
