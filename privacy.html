Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Core principle: WA Exporter is designed so WhatsApp chat, contact and export content is processed locally in the browser. The publisher does not operate a server that receives your exported WhatsApp conversations or contact lists.

1. Information WA Exporter can access

When you use WA Exporter on WhatsApp Web, the extension may read content that is already rendered or made available in your signed-in WhatsApp Web session, including chat messages, timestamps, sender names, chat or group names, visible contact identifiers, and visible group-member information. This access is used only to perform export actions you initiate.

2. Local processing

Chat and contact extraction, formatting and export generation are performed locally in your browser. Exported files are saved through Chrome when you request an export. WA Exporter does not upload your WhatsApp messages, contact lists or generated export files to a publisher-controlled cloud service.

3. Local extension storage

The extension may use Chrome extension storage for settings and operational data such as UI preferences, export options, interrupted-export resume state, free-tier usage counters and PRO activation status. WhatsApp chat content is not intended to be permanently stored in extension storage.

4. Licensing and payment services

If PRO licensing is enabled, WA Exporter may communicate with a third-party payment or licensing provider for license activation, validation, purchase status or related licensing functions. Only licensing-related information should be transmitted for this purpose. WhatsApp chat or contact content is not sent to the licensing provider.

5. Website data

This static website does not intentionally use advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Your browser and hosting provider may process standard technical request information required to deliver the website.

6. Data sale and advertising

WA Exporter does not sell WhatsApp chat or contact content, does not use that content for advertising, and does not use it for creditworthiness or lending decisions.

7. Permissions

The extension may request access to web.whatsapp.com and Chrome permissions needed for user-requested downloads, local settings and interaction with the active WhatsApp Web tab. These permissions are used to provide the export functionality.

8. Security

No software can guarantee absolute security. WA Exporter is designed to minimize unnecessary data transfer and keep the core export pipeline local to the browser.

9. Children

WA Exporter is not specifically directed to children. Users should comply with the terms and age requirements of WhatsApp, Chrome and any applicable services.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated as the product, licensing provider or distribution model evolves. The current version will be published at this URL.

11. Independence

WA Exporter is an independent product and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by WhatsApp LLC or Meta Platforms, Inc. WhatsApp is a trademark of its respective owner.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, use the support information published on the Support page.
