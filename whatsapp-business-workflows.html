Clear answers

Questions worth asking before you export.

Can I export all WhatsApp business chats at once?

WA Exporter PRO supports multi-chat exports. What can be exported depends on the conversations and history available in your authenticated WhatsApp Web session, selected settings and supported product capabilities.

Does it export every image, video and attachment?

Media availability depends on what is accessible in WhatsApp Web and supported by the export workflow. Do not assume a complete media archive without checking the output.

Can WA Exporter train my AI chatbot?

No. WA Exporter helps you export conversation data. You can then prepare appropriately authorized data for separate AI tools or training pipelines.

Can I export WhatsApp contacts directly to Excel?

WA Exporter PRO supports CSV and vCard contact export workflows. CSV files open in Excel; native .xlsx export is not claimed.

Can I use exported group contacts for marketing?

Only where you have the necessary permission and a lawful basis to contact people. Access to a group or an exported phone number does not automatically provide marketing consent.

Is my chat content sent to the payment provider?

WA Exporter uses local-first export processing. Its existing product information states that chat and contact export content is not sent to Dodo Payments; checkout and licensing are separate processes.
